## Descripción del Video
```text
Voxel51 and Campuslands brought the AI, ML, and computer vision community together for two back-to-back events in Bucaramanga:

🔹 Bucaramanga AI, ML, and CV Meetup
An evening of talks on computer vision, AI agents, applied AI workflows, startup building, and the future of software development.

🔹 Data Agents: Visual Agents Hackathon
A hands-on hackathon where students, developers, and builders created autonomous visual agents using the open source FiftyOne ecosystem — working with real datasets, plugins, MCP, and applied computer vision tools.

Across both events, we saw what makes this community special: curiosity, technical ambition, and a real desire to build.

We were also honored to welcome the mayor of Bucaramanga, whose presence underscored the importance of supporting local builders, students, and emerging AI talent.

We’re excited to keep supporting AI and computer vision communities around the world — and Bucaramanga set the bar high.
```
