### Texto Corrido Íntegro
Welcome to Timú. Located in the northeast of Colombia, this region has been marked by the violence and illegality for decades. The presence of armed groups outside of the law and the economy based on cocaine cultivation has defied the reality of this region. However, regardless of this adverse panorama, there is hope. Capuzlan is a social mobility center that focuses on providing opportunities to young people from low-income families and with no previous experience on programming. Throughout an intensive program that includes software development, soft skills, and English, we aim to achieve a successful rate in employability of young people in national and international companies. With a goal of surpassing 80% upon completion of the training, 70% of the people that finish our training works remotely from the places of residency, while the other 30% works in person in different cities across the country. This side is now a reality. It was only possible due to the joining forces of Uniminuto and Capuzlan at the moment we have 70 young people that is receiving the training. And it is because of these stories that we want you to join us and be part of this transformation so we can have more coverage in this region and have more people benefit of the training and get the jobs in the IT sector of the future.
