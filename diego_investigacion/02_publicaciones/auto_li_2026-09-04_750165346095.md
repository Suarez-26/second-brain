## Contenido Íntegro

Yesterday, the U.S. Chamber of Commerce's U.S.-Colombia Business Council (USCBC) and Abel Torres from GRC convened policymakers, business leaders, and digital economy stakeholders in Cartagena for our 2nd Annual Colombia Digital Policy Dialogue — an afternoon of conversations on connectivity, innovation, digital inclusion, and Colombia's digital future. 🇨🇴🇺🇸
 
We were honored to welcome H.E. Alexandra Falla Zerrate, Minister of ICT, FCC Commissioner Olivia Trusty, and members of the Colombian Senate, alongside U.S. government representatives from the U.S. Embassy Bogotá, Colombia and the U.S. Department of Commerce🏛️
 
Panel discussions explored how public policy and private sector collaboration can drive digital growth, expand opportunity for MSMEs, and strengthen Colombia's global competitiveness. 💡

Thank you to our sponsors Amazon, JPMorganChase, Google, and Spotify, and to our partners AmCham Colombia - Cámara de Comercio Colombo Americana and the U.S. Embassy Bogotá, Colombia 🤝
 
We look forward to returning to Cartagena in 2027!
