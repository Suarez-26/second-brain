Building Connections, Inspiring Futures: Mentorship in Action at Western Women in Business Group

Last Monday evening , I was asked to join the Western Women in Business Group as a panelist at Main Campus. The room was filled with 60 incredible young women — part of a community that’s grown to over 300 members in just one year which is truly remarkable! What moved me mostly was that each of the 60 women in attendance because they wanted to learn more about the very career I am most passionate about and that they took their own personal time to educate and engage. 
We explored the role of a wealth advisor, the evolving world of wealth management, and the deeper purpose behind it all: connecting with people and helping them thrive.
What truly stood out was the energy and curiosity in the room — a powerful reminder of why these conversations matter. It re-energizes me each and every time because for me, it is my passion - helping clients and their families and in knowing that some day there will be the next generation of Advisors who have the same commitment and dedication.

If you’re passionate about mentorship, financial wellness, or empowering women in business, connect with me or share your thoughts below. Together, we can inspire the next generation of leaders.
Thank you to the organizers and everyone who made the evening so special Western Women in Business, Diego Tarazona Kate Pedisic
