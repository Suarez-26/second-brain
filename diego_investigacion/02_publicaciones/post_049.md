I am truly impressed by the achievements and outcomes of our Coding Queendom Program, whose essence was to answer : how to reconcile business with social impact ?  

Designed to empower Women in Tech in Amaris Consulting Colombia, this initiative offered young women from underprivileged backgrounds the opportunity to enrol in a six-month training program to acquire IT skills. Alongside a dedicated mentor and free of charge for our valued partners during the training period, their competencies exceeded expectations, leading all of them to continue their professional journey as Consultants. A testament to this initiative's success is reflected in the words of our partner, Sistecredito:  

“This decision is based on her outstanding performance and proactivity demonstrated during her training. We are very pleased and confident that she will continue to make a significant contribution to our company” 

With the initial success of the project, the program has expanded to 16 talented women! 

Thanks to Clément MEMAIN, our teams in Colombia and our valued partners for their trust in this Diversity & Inclusion initiative. Most importantly, a heartfelt congratulations to all the women who took part in the program for their impressive achievements.
